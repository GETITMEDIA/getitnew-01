01. Introduction

Getit Media Solutions Pvt Ltd ("we", "us", "our", or "the Company") respects the privacy of its employees and authorized personnel and is committed to handling personal and employment-related information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy describes what information is collected, accessed, stored, and processed through the Getit Media Solutions application, how that information is used, who may access it, how long it is kept, and how you can contact us with privacy questions or requests.

This policy applies to the Getit Media Solutions application and to information handled through it. By using the application with credentials issued by the Company, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

02. About Getit Media Solutions

The organization responsible for the Getit Media Solutions application is:

Getit Media Solutions Pvt Ltd
No. 10, 2nd Floor, 4th Cross, Victoria Nagar,
Puducherry – 605005, India

Getit Media Solutions Pvt Ltd is a digital marketing and creative services company providing search engine optimization, Google My Business and local SEO, online advertising, social media marketing, website design and development, graphic design, video editing, and related digital services.

Getit Media Solutions Pvt Ltd is the entity that controls and is responsible for information processed through the application.

03. About the Getit Media Solutions Application

Getit Media Solutions is an internal company application developed for use by Getit Media Solutions Pvt Ltd.

The application supports day-to-day internal and administrative activities of the Company, which may include:

  • Secure sign-in for employees and authorized personnel.
  • Viewing and maintaining employee profile information.
  • Applying for and managing leave and permission requests.
  • Viewing payslip and payroll-related documents made available by the Company.
  • Receiving notifications and internal communications from the Company.

The application is not a public consumer service, a marketplace, or a social platform. It exists solely to support the internal operations of Getit Media Solutions Pvt Ltd.

04. Who Can Use the Application

Use of the Getit Media Solutions application is restricted to:

  • Current employees of Getit Media Solutions Pvt Ltd.
  • Authorized personnel of the Company, such as HR and management staff.

The application is not intended for public account registration and is not available for use by the general public. Members of the public cannot sign up for or create an account.

Access depends on your role within the Company and your employment status. If your employment or authorization ends, your access may be withdrawn or disabled.

05. How User Accounts Are Created and Managed

Employees do not create their own accounts in the Getit Media Solutions application.

  • Accounts are created and managed by authorized HR or management personnel of the Company.
  • Employees receive organization-managed login credentials from the Company.
  • Account details, roles, and permissions are configured and maintained by authorized personnel.
  • Accounts may be updated, suspended, deactivated, or removed by authorized personnel based on role changes or employment status.

You are responsible for keeping the credentials issued to you confidential and for informing the Company if you believe your account has been accessed without authorization.

06. Information We Collect

We only collect and process information that is necessary for the internal, administrative, HR, and payroll purposes described in this policy.

Depending on your role and how the application is used, the following categories of information may be collected, accessed, stored, or processed:

  • Account and authentication information – such as the email address or identifier used to sign in and authentication records maintained by Firebase Authentication.
  • Employee and employment-related information – as described in the section below.
  • Leave and permission information – requests, dates, reasons where provided, and approval status.
  • Payslip and payroll-related information – documents and records made available to you by the Company.
  • Profile images and documents – only where you or authorized personnel upload them.
  • Device and notification information – such as the notification token required to deliver push notifications.
  • Application usage records – such as records of requests submitted or actions taken within the application, used for internal administration and record-keeping.

The application does not collect precise location data, contacts, microphone recordings, biometric information, advertising identifiers, or payment card details.

07. Employee and Employment-Related Information

The application may hold employment-related information provided or maintained by the Company, which may include:

  • Name and employee identifier.
  • Official contact details such as work email address and phone number.
  • Designation, department, or role within the Company.
  • Date of joining and employment status.
  • Reporting or approval relationships used for leave and permission workflows.

This information is entered or maintained by authorized HR or management personnel, or updated by employees where the application permits.

It is used only for legitimate internal business, HR, payroll, communication, security, and administrative purposes.

08. Leave and Permission Information

Where the application is used to manage leave and permission requests, it may process:

  • The type of leave or permission requested.
  • Requested dates, times, and duration.
  • Any reason or description you choose to provide with the request.
  • The status of the request, such as pending, approved, or rejected.
  • Records of approvals or actions taken by authorized approvers.
  • Leave balances and related records maintained by the Company.

This information is visible to you and to the authorized personnel responsible for reviewing, approving, and recording such requests.

09. Payslip and Payroll-Related Information

Where the application is used to make payslips available, it may process payroll-related information such as payslip documents and associated records prepared by the Company.

Payslip documents may be generated, viewed, downloaded, or shared from within the application where that functionality is provided.

Payslip and payroll information is confidential. Access is restricted to you and to authorized personnel who require access for payroll, accounting, HR, audit, or compliance purposes.

If you choose to share a payslip document outside the application using your device, that sharing is under your control and is no longer governed by this policy.

10. Profile Images and Documents

Where the application supports it, a profile image or supporting document may be uploaded by you or by authorized personnel.

  • Uploaded images and documents are stored using Firebase Storage.
  • They are used to display your profile within the application and to maintain employment records where applicable.
  • Where an image is selected from your device, only the file you choose is uploaded.

If the application does not provide upload functionality for your role, no such information is collected from you.

11. Device and Notification Information

The application uses Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver push notifications relating to internal matters, such as updates on leave or permission requests, payroll availability, or Company announcements.

To make this possible, the application may process:

  • A notification token issued to your app installation, used to route notifications to your device.
  • Basic technical information required for reliable delivery of notifications and for the application to function correctly.

You can disable notifications at any time through your device settings. Disabling notifications may mean you no longer receive timely updates from the application.

12. How We Use Information

Information processed through the application is used only for legitimate internal purposes, including:

  • Authenticating users and providing secure access to the application.
  • Maintaining accurate employee and employment records.
  • Processing, reviewing, approving, and recording leave and permission requests.
  • Preparing and making available payslip and payroll-related documents.
  • Sending internal notifications and communications.
  • Administering roles, permissions, and access within the application.
  • Maintaining the security and integrity of the application and Company information.
  • Supporting internal administration, record-keeping, audit, and compliance requirements.
  • Meeting applicable legal, statutory, and regulatory obligations.

We do not use information from the application for advertising, marketing profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the internal operation of the Company.

13. Role-Based Access and Internal Use

The application may use role-based access, such as Employee, HR, and Manager roles. Different roles have different permissions and different levels of access to Company information.

  • Employee – can generally access their own profile, requests, and documents.
  • Manager – may additionally access information required to review and act on requests from reporting employees.
  • HR / authorized management personnel – may access employee, leave, and payroll-related information as required to perform HR, payroll, and administrative duties.

Roles and permissions are assigned and maintained by authorized personnel of the Company. Access is granted on the basis of business need and job responsibilities.

14. Firebase and Other Service Providers

The application uses Google Firebase services to operate. These services process information on our behalf to provide the functionality described in this policy:

  • Firebase Authentication – for sign-in, authentication, and account access.
  • Cloud Firestore – for storing and retrieving application data such as employee records, leave and permission requests, and related information.
  • Firebase Storage – for storing files and images, such as profile images and documents, where applicable.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging – for delivering push notifications.

These providers process information in accordance with their own terms and privacy practices and are used strictly to operate the application.

We do not sell information, and we do not share information with third parties for their own advertising or marketing purposes.

15. Data Storage

Application data is stored using Google Firebase infrastructure, including Cloud Firestore for application data and Firebase Storage for files and images where applicable.

Authentication records are maintained through Firebase Authentication.

Because these are cloud-based services operated by Google, information may be stored and processed on servers located in one or more regions where those services operate.

Some information may also be held in the Company’s own internal HR, payroll, and administrative records outside the application, in accordance with normal business practice.

16. Data Sharing and Authorized Access

Information in the application is treated as confidential Company information. It may be accessed by:

  • The employee to whom the information relates.
  • Authorized HR and management personnel who require access to perform their duties.
  • Approvers within the relevant workflow, for the requests they are responsible for reviewing.
  • Service providers that operate the application infrastructure, as described in the Firebase section above.

Information may also be disclosed where we are required to do so by applicable law, regulation, legal process, or a lawful request from a competent authority, or where necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of the Company and its personnel.

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information, and we do not disclose it to third parties for unrelated purposes.

17. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information in the application against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, disclosure, or loss. These measures may include:

  • Authenticated access using organization-managed credentials.
  • Role-based permissions that limit access to what is needed for a user’s role.
  • Use of established cloud infrastructure with security controls provided by Firebase and Google Cloud.
  • Encrypted transmission of data between the application and Firebase services.
  • Restricting administrative access to authorized personnel.

No method of transmission over the internet and no method of electronic storage can be considered completely secure. While we work to protect information using reasonable safeguards, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Users are expected to keep their credentials confidential, use secure devices, and report any suspected security incident to the Company promptly.

18. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as it is reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy.

Retention periods depend on the nature of the information and may be influenced by:

  • Ongoing employment and the need to provide access to the application.
  • HR, payroll, accounting, and administrative record-keeping requirements.
  • Legal, statutory, tax, audit, or regulatory obligations.
  • The need to resolve disputes, enforce agreements, or protect legal rights.
  • Security and internal governance requirements.

Certain employment, payroll, and statutory records must be retained for defined periods even after employment ends. When information is no longer required, it is deleted or otherwise dealt with in accordance with Company practice and applicable law.

19. Account Deactivation and Data Deletion Requests

Because accounts in the Getit Media Solutions application are created and managed by authorized HR or management personnel, employees cannot delete their own accounts from within the application.

To request deactivation of your account, or deletion of information where applicable, please contact your HR department or the privacy contact listed in the "Contact Us" section of this policy.

When a request is received:

  • We may need to verify your identity and your employment or authorization status.
  • Account access can generally be disabled or removed so the account can no longer be used.
  • Information that is no longer required will be deleted or removed in accordance with Company practice.

Please note that account deactivation does not automatically delete all associated records. Employment, payroll, statutory, audit, and other records may need to be retained for legitimate business, legal, security, payroll, or compliance purposes, even after account access has been removed.

20. Employee Privacy Rights and Requests

Subject to applicable law and Company policy, you may:

  • Ask what personal information about you is held in the application.
  • Request correction of information that is inaccurate or out of date.
  • Request deletion of information where deletion is legally and operationally possible.
  • Raise questions or concerns about how your information is handled.
  • Request that your account be deactivated, as described in the section above.

Requests can be made through your HR department or using the contact details in the "Contact Us" section. We may need to verify your identity before acting on certain requests, and we will respond within a reasonable period.

Some requests may be limited where we are required to retain information for legal, payroll, audit, or compliance reasons.

21. Children’s Privacy

The Getit Media Solutions application is an internal workplace application intended solely for employees and authorized personnel of Getit Media Solutions Pvt Ltd.

It is not directed at children and is not intended for use by children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the application.

If you believe information relating to a child has been provided to us in error, please contact us so that we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

22. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the application, our practices, the services we use, or applicable legal requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Effective Date / Last Updated" date at the top.

Where a change is significant, we may also communicate it through the application or through internal Company communication channels. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
